Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026. This is a starter policy — have it reviewed before collecting real applications.

What we collect

When you apply we collect only what the form asks for: full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, preferred industry track, preferred program, and your consent record. We do not collect payments on this page.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your application, confirm your track and schedule, and administer the batch. We do not sell your data.

Where it goes

Online submission is not configured yet (see the integration note on the application form). A valid application opens your own email app with a message pre-addressed to admissions@mtoaccelerator.com — your details are transmitted only if you press send. Once a form provider is connected, it will be named here with its data location and retention period.

Retention and deletion

Application records are kept only as long as needed for admissions and placement support. To request correction or deletion, email admissions@mtoaccelerator.com.

Third parties

This page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Font delivery may expose your IP address to Google. No analytics or advertising trackers are installed in this repository.
