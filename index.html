Course overview

MTO Job Accelerator: From application to assessment centre

Prepare final-year students and fresh graduates for Management Trainee, MTO, Graduate Trainee, Future Leader and Young Leadership positions in Bangladesh.

Recommended duration

  • 8 weeks structured training
  • 3 live classes per week · 24 classes
  • 2 hours per class · 48 live hours
  • 20–25 hours guided practice
  • 90-day placement support

Delivery format

  • Live classes + recorded modules
  • Quizzes + timed aptitude exams
  • Excel and PowerPoint labs
  • Case simulations + group discussion
  • Interview simulations + full assessment centre
  • Feedback sessions + placement support

Target participants

Final-year students, recent graduates, up to two years experience. Banks, FMCG, telecom, tech, manufacturing and large local corporations.

Cohort size: maximum 30 per batch. Language: English with Bangla explanations where necessary.

Two ways to train

Choose your pace: 4-week Masterclass or 8-week Accelerator

Masterclass covers the selection journey. Accelerator adds Excel and PowerPoint labs, business cases, group discussion, digital interviews, full simulation, 1:1 feedback, certification and placement support.

Masterclass · 4 weeks

3 days per week live + recorded modules. Live sessions, mock tests, quizzes, CV and LinkedIn workshop, interview practice.

Week 1: Hiring process + CV and LinkedIn

  • How MTO hiring works
  • ATS-friendly CV with impact bullets
  • LinkedIn optimization and networking

Week 2: Aptitude and logical reasoning

  • Numerical aptitude: percentage, ratio, DI
  • Logical reasoning: series, patterns, syllogism
  • Data interpretation + timed practice

Week 3: Verbal + psychometric and SJT

  • Reading comprehension, critical reasoning
  • Consistent role-fit answers in psychometric and SJT

Week 4: Interview and assessment centre

  • STAR method answers
  • Case study and group discussion
  • Final mock + feedback + 30/60/90-day plan

Accelerator · 8 weeks

Everything in Masterclass, plus Excel and PowerPoint labs, business cases, group discussion, digital interviews, full simulation, 1:1 feedback and placement support.

  • 8 weeks · 24 classes · 48 hours
  • 20–25 hours guided practice
  • 90-day placement support
  • Competency certification + scorecard

Learning outcomes

14 things you will be able to do

  1. Identify suitable MTO programs by background and eligibility.
  2. Prepare an ATS-compatible CV with quantified achievements.
  3. Complete application forms without positioning errors.
  4. Build a recruiter-friendly LinkedIn profile and network.
  5. Solve aptitude questions under strict time limits.
  6. Approach psychometric and SJT tests consistently.

  11. Answer competency interviews with structured evidence.

Eight risks, eight failsafes

  1. Job expectation: readiness guarantee, not 100% job promise.
  2. No practice: mandatory gates and supervised tests.
  3. Outdated content: review 30 ads per batch, quarterly bank update.
  4. One-size course: core plus 4 track labs.
  5. AI work: live defence and supervised tests.
  6. Weak students: diagnostics, remedials, retests, scorecards.
  7. No applications: tracker and accountability built in.
  8. Unverified claims: documented records with consent.
