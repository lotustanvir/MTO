Two ways to train

Choose your pace: 4-week Masterclass or 8-week Accelerator

Masterclass covers the selection journey. Accelerator adds Excel and PowerPoint labs, business cases, group discussion, digital interviews, full simulation, 1:1 feedback, certification and placement support.

Masterclass · 4 weeks

3 days per week live + recorded modules. Live sessions, mock tests, quizzes, CV and LinkedIn workshop, interview practice.

Week 1: Hiring process + CV and LinkedIn

  • How MTO hiring works
  • ATS-friendly CV with impact bullets
  • LinkedIn optimization and networking

Week 2: Aptitude and logical reasoning

  • Numerical aptitude: percentage, ratio, DI
  • Logical reasoning: series, patterns, syllogism
  • Data interpretation + timed practice

Week 3: Verbal + psychometric and SJT

  • Reading comprehension, critical reasoning
  • Consistent role-fit answers in psychometric and SJT

Week 4: Interview and assessment centre

  • STAR method answers
  • Case study and group discussion
  • Final mock + feedback + 30/60/90-day plan

Accelerator · 8 weeks

Everything in Masterclass, plus Excel and PowerPoint labs, business cases, group discussion, digital interviews, full simulation, 1:1 feedback and placement support.

  • 8 weeks · 24 classes · 48 hours
  • 20–25 hours guided practice
  • 90-day placement support
  • Competency certification + scorecard
